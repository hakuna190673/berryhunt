🍓 About Berry Hunt

Berry Hunt is a vibrant 2D retro arcade platformer developed by Hakuna Games. Take control of Jammy the adventurous mouse across multiple handcrafted levels, dodging obstacles, climbing ladders, collecting golden berries, and competing for the highest scores.

  • Fluid Controls: Responsive touch gestures designed natively for iPhone portrait mode.
  • Ad-Free Experience: 100% free of third-party advertisements and tracking.
  • Family Friendly: Suitable and safe for players of all ages.
✨ Built with modern AI: This project was proudly vibecoded.

🛠️ App Support & Contact

Have a question, feedback, or encountered a problem while playing Berry Hunt? We're here to help!

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I jump? Tap anywhere on the screen to jump.
  • How do I move? Swipe left or right to run in that direction. Swipe up/down to climb ladders or stop.
  • How do I reset my High Score or Tutorial? In the game's main menu, tap Settings to find options for resetting the tutorial or high scores.
  • Does the app require an internet connection? No. Berry Hunt runs completely offline on your device.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Privacy Summary: Berry Hunt does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or analytics. The app is completely ad-free and tracking-free.

1. Information Collection and Use

Berry Hunt does not require account creation, registration, or login. We do not collect names, email addresses, location information, or device identifiers.

2. Advertisements & Tracking

Berry Hunt is 100% ad-free. The app does not integrate any advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) or tracking frameworks. We do not track your activity across apps or websites.

3. Local Device Storage

To save your game progress (current level and high score), Berry Hunt uses local on-device storage (iOS UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because Berry Hunt collects no personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K standards.
